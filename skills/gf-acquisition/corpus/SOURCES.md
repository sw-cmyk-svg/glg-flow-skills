## Benchmark priors to anchor (verify at source before citing)

- **Landing-page conversion rate** — median ~2–6%, wide by industry (Unbounce / WordStream).
  *(acquisition-conversion)*
- **CAC payback period** — healthy SaaS commonly **< 12 months** (varies by ACV/segment)
  (OpenView / Bessemer). *(attribution-efficiency)*
- **LTV:CAC** — the **~3:1 rule of thumb** — anchor it honestly as a heuristic, not a law.
  *(attribution-efficiency)*
- **Viral coefficient (k-factor)** — **k > 1 = self-sustaining virality; most products sit k < 1** —
  with the Referral-seam note that *designing* the loop is Referral's, *measuring* the channel is
  Acquisition's. *(channel-strategy / Referral seam)*
