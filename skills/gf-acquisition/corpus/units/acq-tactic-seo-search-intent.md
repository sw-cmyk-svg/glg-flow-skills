**Tactic — win organic by matching content to the searcher's intent, not to keywords, and build authority on a focused topic.**

Organic search rewards content that **answers the actual job** behind a query. The lever is **search
intent**: is the searcher trying to learn (informational), compare options (commercial), or act
(transactional)? A page that matches the intent — and sits inside a cluster of related content that
signals **topical authority** — earns durable ranking and brings in visitors who are already looking
for what you do. For a PLG product, the highest-value organic content usually targets the problem the
product solves (so the signup is a natural next step), not generic high-volume keywords that convert
poorly.

**How to apply.** Start from the problems your best-fit segment searches for, map each to its intent,
and build content that satisfies that intent better than what ranks today — then cluster related pieces
and link them so the topic reads as authoritative. Prioritize fewer, deeper, intent-matched pieces over
broad keyword coverage. Tie the content to a relevant signup/CTA so `unique_visitors` convert, not just
accumulate.

**When it applies.** Standing up or improving an organic/SEO channel; diagnosing content that gets
traffic but no signups (usually an intent or audience mismatch).

**Caution (honesty).** SEO is a **slow, compounding** channel with a long lead time and it depends on
search-engine algorithms you don't control — don't promise rankings or timelines. Manipulative or
black-hat tactics (keyword stuffing, cloaking, link schemes, thin AI-spun pages) are anti-patterns:
they risk penalties and erode trust, and the AI-overview shift is squeezing low-value content hardest.
