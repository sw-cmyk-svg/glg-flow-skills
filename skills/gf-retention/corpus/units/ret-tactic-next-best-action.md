**Tactic — surface the one relevant next step, tailored to this user, at the right moment.**

Users don't retain by being shown everything; they retain by being shown the **right next thing** for
where they are. A next-best-action surface (a recommended template, the feature that fits their
workflow, the content that follows what they just did) reduces the "what now?" gap that leaks
retention. Done well it's personalization in service of momentum, not noise.

**How to apply.** Rank candidate next steps by relevance to the user's current behaviour and goal,
surface **one or a few** (not a wall of options), and place them where the user already is. Prefer
actions that lead to a value moment over actions that just drive clicks. Measure whether recommended
actions actually get taken and whether they move retention — retire ones that don't.

**When it applies.** Products with enough depth that users can't see the best next step unaided;
content/catalog products where a relevant recommendation keeps the session going.

**Caution (ethics).** Optimize recommendations for the **user's** value, not just engagement-for-its-
own-sake — ranking purely for time-on-app or clicks drifts into attention-hooking and filter-bubble
territory (documented, not prescribed). Be transparent about why something is recommended, and keep it
within the consented data boundary. For bank/wallet and betting, never recommend actions that nudge
spending or wagering. Pairs with [[ret-tactic-guide-to-value-continuously]] and
[[ret-principle-relevance-decay]].
