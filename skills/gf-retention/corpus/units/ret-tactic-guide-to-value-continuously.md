**Tactic — keep guiding users to value after onboarding, not just during it.**

Retention isn't only an onboarding problem. Pendo's framing: *improving retention revolves around
guidance* — simplify onboarding, **continue to add value** (nurture users even after they've got the
hang of it, with in-app messaging that helps them keep uncovering features at their own pace), and
use analytics to **guide at-risk users back to value**. A Jungle Scout CX lead credits bringing
"learning and help at the right points" for keeping customers and deepening their use.

**How to apply.** Don't stop the helpful cues at activation. Surface the **next useful feature** at
the moment it's relevant, point declining/at-risk users back to the value they're missing, and make
discovery **ongoing**. Learn what the most engaged users do, then guide others toward the same.

**When it applies.** Products with more depth than a new user discovers at first.

**Caution.** Guidance must be **relevant and well-timed, not nagging** (ties to the Fogg prompt types
and the notification ethics caution in [[ret-principle-fogg-behavior-model]]). And note the source
bias: **Pendo sells in-app guidance**, so treat "guidance solves retention" as directional, not a
neutral finding. Pairs with [[ret-principle-feature-adoption-breadth]].
