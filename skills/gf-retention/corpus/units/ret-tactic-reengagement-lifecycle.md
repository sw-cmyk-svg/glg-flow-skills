**Tactic — bring lapsing users back with timely, relevant lifecycle messages.**

A lapsing user often just needs a well-timed cue pointed at real value — the Fogg **prompt**
([[ret-principle-fogg-behavior-model]]). Lifecycle messaging triggers email / push / in-app messages
off behaviour (inactive N days, abandoned a key action, missed a value moment) to pull users back —
but only works when the message **leads to something worth returning for**, not a generic "we miss
you". Segment by behaviour and lifecycle stage; make the reason to return specific to the user.

**How to apply.** Define the lapsing signals, trigger a relevant message that routes straight to
value, cap frequency, and honour consent and unsubscribe. Test what actually recovers users, and
retire messages that don't.

**When it applies.** Products with a return cadence and a way to reach lapsing users (email, push).

**Caution (ethics) — strong here.** Consent, frequency, and relevance are the line. Notification
spam, manipulative urgency, guilt mechanics ("your streak is dying!"), and hard-to-unsubscribe flows
are dark patterns — documented, never prescribed — and they also breach anti-spam / platform
notification rules. For bank/wallet and betting, re-engagement must never nudge spending or wagering
(responsible-use / responsible-gambling caution). Pairs with [[ret-tactic-detect-churn-signals-early]].
