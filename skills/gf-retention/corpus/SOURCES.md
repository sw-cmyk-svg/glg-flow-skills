# Retention corpus — sources

Public sources for the Retention specialist's corpus (business_model: `plg-saas`). Units **distil**
these in GL's own words and **cite** them — never reproduce them. Verification is part of drafting:
a benchmark/case-study figure is cited only after it is verified at source; second-hand figures are
attributed to the proximate source and flagged. Same curation rules as `corpus/activation/`.

## Ethics note

Retention is the stage where compulsion and dark patterns live (variable-reward loops, streak
pressure, manipulative notifications, false urgency). Every persuasion-adjacent unit carries a
mandatory `Caution (ethics):` line; regulated archetypes (bank/wallet, betting) get a compliance
caution (not a ban); dark patterns are documented only as anti-patterns. Mirrors the Activation
curation principle and `copilot/GUARDRAILS.md`.
