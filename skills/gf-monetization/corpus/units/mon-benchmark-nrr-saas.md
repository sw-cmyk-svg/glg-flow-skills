**Benchmark prior — median net revenue retention is ~110% for public SaaS.**

Reference point for NRR: **median NRR hovers around 110%** for public SaaS companies in the 2024
benchmark set. Best-in-class PLG companies run higher (commonly ~120%+); companies with no expansion
motion sit at or below 100%. The same 2024 context shows growth has cooled broadly (median public-SaaS
year-on-year growth fell from 30–40% in 2021–22 to ~15% in 2024), so expansion carries more of the
growth load than it used to.

**How to use.** Anchor the client's NRR against ~110% median: above it, expansion is working; around
100%, expansion is barely offsetting churn; below 100%, the base is shrinking and that's the priority.
Always read NRR alongside **logo/revenue churn** — a good NRR can mask bad churn in a few big accounts.

**Definition caveat.** NRR definitions vary (monthly vs annual, cohort windows, whether downgrades and
partial churn are netted). Confirm the client computes it the same way before comparing to the 110%
figure. The number is a prior, not a verdict.
