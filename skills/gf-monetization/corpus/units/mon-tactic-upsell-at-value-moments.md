**Tactic — make the upgrade ask at the moment the customer hits a value limit, not on a sales calendar.**

The best expansion prompt is triggered by **behaviour**, not a quarterly push: the customer is about to
hit a usage cap, needs a seat for a new teammate, or just reached for a feature in a higher tier. At
that moment the value is self-evident and the upgrade feels like unlocking, not being sold to. In-product
prompts tied to real limits convert better than generic "upgrade now" banners or cold outreach.

**How to apply.** Instrument the value-metric limits and the power-feature reaches, and surface a
specific, contextual upgrade at those points ("you've used 90% of your included runs — add more" / "add
a seat for the person you're inviting"). Route high-value accounts to sales-assist where the deal
warrants it. Measure expansion per trigger and keep the ones that actually convert and retain.

**When it applies.** Products with usage/seat limits or tiered power features; land-and-expand motions.

**Caution (ethics).** Trigger on **real** limits and real value, never a manufactured wall or a limit
quietly lowered to force upgrades. Don't nag — one well-placed, relevant prompt beats constant banners.
