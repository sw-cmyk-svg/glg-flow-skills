**Tactic — put the paywall *after* the value moment, not before it.**

When the paywall sits decides whether it converts or kills. A paywall placed **before the user has felt
the value** blocks activation and reads as a toll gate — that's an Activation problem, and it suppresses
the very conversion you want. Placed **at the moment the user has just felt the value and wants more**
(hit a usage limit, finished a first win, needs to invite a teammate), the ask lands when willingness
to pay is highest.

**How to apply.** Map the user's path to the value moment, let them reach it free, then present the
upgrade at the natural "I want more of this" trigger — tied to the value metric (more usage, more
seats, the power feature they just reached for). Show what crossing the line unlocks in concrete terms
at that moment, not a generic "go Pro."

**When it applies.** Freemium and trial conversion design; fixing a product where users bounce at a
pricing wall before activating.

**Caution (seam + ethics).** If the paywall blocks *first value entirely*, that's an **Activation**
decision, not a monetization win — route it. Don't dark-pattern the moment with fake scarcity or a
countdown; the trigger should be the real value limit. Pairs with [[mon-tactic-feature-gating]] and
[[mon-tactic-model-choice]].
