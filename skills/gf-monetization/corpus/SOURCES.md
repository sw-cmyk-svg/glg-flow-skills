# Monetization corpus — sources

**Business model:** `plg-saas` (proof archetype). Same curation rules as Activation/Retention:
GL-authored distilled note + citation (transformative / copyright-safe, never reproduces source text),
I-draft-you-vet, verification-as-drafting, and the persuasion-ethics principle (every
persuasion-adjacent unit carries a mandatory `Caution (ethics):` line; pricing dark patterns are
documented only as anti-patterns).

## In-hand (carry over from Activation/Retention)

- **The book** — Shotton & Flicker, *Hacking the Human Mind* (Macmillan, 2025). Monetization-relevant
  chapters: ch.5 **charm pricing** (Amazon Prime), ch.8 **price relativity / price as a badge of
  quality / costly signaling** (Red Bull), ch.13 **present bias / temporal reframing** (Klarna). Used
  for price-setting + free-to-paid psychology, each with its ethics caution.
- **Balfour (Reforge)** — growth-model framing; monetization as part of the engine. Strategy spine.
- **Pendo / Userpilot / Amplitude** PDFs — some paywall / upgrade-prompt and expansion material.

## To mine (public, SaaS-native)

- **ProfitWell / Paddle (Patrick Campbell)** — pricing research, **value-metric** pricing, freemium vs
  trial conversion benchmarks, price-localization, churn/dunning (involuntary churn). First-party-ish
  pricing data at scale.
- **OpenView / Kyle Poyar** — PLG monetization: **usage-based pricing**, **net revenue retention (NRR)**
  benchmarks, freemium→paid conversion, the expansion playbook.
- **Reforge (Elena Verna, Patrick Campbell)** — monetization model, freemium vs free trial vs reverse
  trial, PLG → sales-assist.
- **Lenny's Newsletter** — pricing & packaging teardowns, willingness-to-pay.
- **Van Westendorp Price Sensitivity Meter** — public method for willingness-to-pay research.

## Benchmark priors to anchor (verify at source before citing)

- **Freemium → paid** conversion (~2–5% typical; wide spread) — ProfitWell / OpenView.
- **Free trial → paid** conversion (~15–25% opt-in; higher for opt-out/CC-required) — ProfitWell.
- **Net revenue retention (NRR)** (~100% median SaaS, ~120%+ best-in-class PLG) — OpenView.
- **Involuntary (failed-payment) churn** share of total churn (often 20–40%) — ProfitWell / Paddle.
