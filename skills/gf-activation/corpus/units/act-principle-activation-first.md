**Principle — work on activation first; the first day is the window.**

Activation is the highest-leverage place to start: Elena Verna's framing is to **start with
activation**, because lifting it tends to pull up monetization, retention, and acquisition too.
Kyle Poyar's self-serve onboarding work makes the urgency concrete: the **first day is the most
critical part of the journey**, and at the average SaaS company **40–60%+ of new users never
return on day 2**. Users who reach value on day one are far more likely to pay and to refer, and
better activation lifts free-to-paid conversion, retention, net expansion, CAC payback, and NPS.

**How to apply (Recommend).** Before anything else, make sure new users reach a real value moment
**fast — ideally day one**. Sequence growth work to fix activation before pouring budget into
acquisition on top of a leaky first-run.

**When it applies.** Prioritization / sequencing decisions in the Recommend step.
