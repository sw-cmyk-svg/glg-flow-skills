**Benchmark — reaching value at or before signup pays off (second-hand figures).**

Figures cited by Chameleon from third parties:
- **31%** of SaaS companies now offer a **pre-signup interactive demo**, up from **17% in 2023**
  (Navattic) — the practice is going mainstream.
- **+65%** trial conversion with a pre-signup interactive demo (Arcade Software).
- **+30%** activation for products with **early onboarding touchpoints** (Product-Led Alliance).

**How to apply (Recommend/Design).** Evidence to support showing value **before** the signup
wall and front-loading the first touchpoint — the quantified backing for
[[act-tactic-value-before-signup]].

**When it applies.** Signup-stage design decisions, especially for products where users can see
value without an account.

**Caution.** **All three figures are second-hand** (Chameleon quoting Navattic / Arcade /
Product-Led Alliance) — attributed to the proximate source and **flagged for original-source
confirmation** before using as hard benchmarks. Definitions (trial conversion, "activation")
vary by source and may not equal the spec's `activation_rate`.
