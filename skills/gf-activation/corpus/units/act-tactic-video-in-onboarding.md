**Tactic — a short welcome video / GIF in onboarding (correlated with high activation).**

Userpilot looked at the onboarding of its **top activators** and found a pattern: **~80% of
companies with an activation rate above 50% included a video, GIF, or animated image** in their
new-user flow. A cited Wyzowl survey adds that **97%** of people consider video an effective way
to welcome and educate new customers.

**How to apply.** A brief, skippable welcome video or animated demo — showing the product
delivering value, not a feature tour — is a low-cost onboarding addition to test, especially for
products whose value is hard to convey in static UI.

**When it applies.** First-run / empty state, particularly for products with a non-obvious "aha."

**Caution (causal honesty).** This is **correlational, not causal** — top activators *also* tend
to do many things well, so video may be a marker of mature onboarding rather than the cause of
high activation. Don't present "add a video → +activation" as proven; treat it as a cheap
hypothesis to validate (see Validate branch-selection). Keep it short and skippable so it doesn't
add friction ([[act-principle-make-it-easy-friction]]). Pairs with
[[act-design-embedded-prompt-starters]].
