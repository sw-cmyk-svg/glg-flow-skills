**Benchmark — onboarding checklist completion rate ≈ 19% (first-party, 2025).**

Across **188** SaaS companies, the average **onboarding checklist completion rate is ~19%** —
i.e. roughly four in five users who see a checklist don't finish it. This is the SaaS-native
benchmark for the checklist/progress mechanics in [[act-tactic-endowed-progress]] and
[[act-design-daisy-chained-checklists]].

**How to apply.** Treat ~19% as the baseline a plain checklist earns; the design levers that beat
it are **short + staged** (Appcues 2%→25% via daisy-chaining), **progress indicators** (+~12%
completion, Chameleon), and **branching on experience** (Hotjar +26% on the key step). A client
far below 19% has a checklist-design problem; far above means the checklist is doing real work.

**When it applies.** Any onboarding that uses a checklist; sizing the opportunity from a poor
completion rate.

**Caution.** Checklist completion is a **proxy**, not the spec's `activation_rate` — a user can
finish a checklist without activating, or activate without it. Confirm the link to real
activation on the client's funnel (causal-vs-correlational honesty). First-party vendor data, so
it skews toward companies already using onboarding tooling.
