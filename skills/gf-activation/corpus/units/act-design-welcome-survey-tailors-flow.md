**Case study — Kontentino: a welcome survey that tailors the flow by role & goal.**

Kontentino (social-media management) replaced linear tours with **interactive walkthroughs**, an
**onboarding checklist**, tooltips/driven actions, and a **welcome survey that tailors the flow
by role and goal**. Both activation metrics — **linking a social account** and **scheduling the
first post** — rose by **~10% within the first month**.

**The design lesson.** A welcome survey earns its place only when it **changes the subsequent
flow** (which walkthrough, which first action), and it works best **paired with in-product
guidance** toward the tailored first action — profiling plus a nudge, not profiling alone. This
is the SaaS-native corroboration of [[act-tactic-segment-at-signup]] and
[[act-design-branch-checklist-by-experience]].

**When it applies.** Multi-use-case products where role/goal changes what "first value" is.

**Caution (causal honesty).** The ~10% lift is from a **bundle** (walkthroughs + checklist +
survey + tooltips) measured over the first month — it is **not isolated** to the welcome survey,
so don't attribute the gain to personalization alone. Keep the survey short
([[act-principle-make-it-easy-friction]]).
