**Framework — read where the problem sits before fixing it.**

A quick triage (illustrative shape, not benchmarks):
- **Many sign up, few activate** (e.g. 70% sign up, 20% activate) → an **onboarding**
  problem: value isn't being reached. Fix the signup→aha path.
- **Many activate, few stay** (e.g. 20% activate, 5% at month 3) → a **long-term value**
  problem: the product delivers a first hit but not a reason to return. Fix core value /
  habit, not onboarding.

**How to apply (Analyze step).** Find the biggest drop — signup→activation vs
activation→retention — and route the work accordingly. Don't polish onboarding when the
leak is retention.

**Caution.** The percentages above show the *shape* of the diagnosis, not benchmarks; always
use the client's own funnel. Pairs with [[act-benchmark-activation-predicts-retention]].
