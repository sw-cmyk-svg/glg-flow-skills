## Benchmarks  *(feeds Benchmark)*
- **Produktly — SaaS Onboarding Benchmarks 2026** *(founder)* — https://produktly.com/research/saas-onboarding-benchmarks-2026 — ✅ verified 2026-11-17. **Scope note:** reports product-**tour completion**, not activation rate; 464 companies, Jan–Jun 2026; SMB-skewed, enterprise underrepresented.
- **Growth Unhinged — benchmark posts** *(founder)*
- **High Alpha — SaaS Benchmarks** *(founder)* — ⚠️ https://www.highalpha.com/saas-benchmarks now 301-redirects to https://visible.vc/startup-benchmarks (data moved; re-verify at new URL).
- **Amplitude — 2026 Product Benchmark Report** *(founder provided the PDF)* — https://amplitude.com/resources/2026-product-benchmark — ✅ verified 2026-11-17 from the report (2,500+ companies). **Definition caveat:** Amplitude "activation" = share returning on a specified day after first visit (day-N return), *not* the spec's event-within-7-days `activation_rate` — cite with this distinction.
- **Mixpanel — 2025 Fintech benchmarks** *(founder)*
- **Chameleon — User Onboarding Benchmark Report 2025** *(founder)* — ✅ verified 2026-11-17 (550M+ interactions, platform data): tour completion ~61%, progress indicators +12%/−20% dismissal, self-serve +123%, >5 steps loses half. Its "The Future" stats are **2ndary** (Navattic/Arcade/Product-Led Alliance) → flagged.
- **Appcues — Hotjar personalized onboarding case** *(founder)* — ✅ verified 2026-11-17: experience-branched checklists, **+26% install** (relative; no absolutes).
- **Appcues GoodUX — bite-sized/daisy-chained checklists** *(founder)* — ✅ verified 2026-11-17: staged unlocking checklists, completion **2%→25%** (the "−20% activation" show-note excluded as a likely wording error, not checklist-attributed).
- **Userpilot — user onboarding case studies** *(founder)* — ✅ verified 2026-11-17 (8 cases w/ figures: Kontentino welcome-survey +~10% activation; Sked 3× checklist; Platformly >40%; etc.). Not all drafted yet.
- **Userpilot — SaaS Product Metrics Benchmark Report 2025** *(founder, PDF)* — ✅ verified 2026-11-17, **first-party, 547 SaaS companies** (segmented by size / vertical / PLG-vs-SLG). Flagship for activation: avg **37.5%** / median 37% (62 cos; ≈ Lenny's 36%), TTV **~1d 12h**, checklist completion **~19%** (188 cos), by-industry (AI&ML 54.8% → FinTech 5%), ~80% of >50%-activation cos use onboarding video (correlational). **Caveat:** Userpilot "activation" has no fixed window (spec = within 7 days); TTV is a mean (spec = median). Pages 19–90 (TTV/retention/NPS/how-to-improve, by industry+size) not yet mined.
- **Pendo × Mind the Product — Global Product Benchmarks (MTP)** *(founder, PDF)* — ✅ verified 2026-11-17, **first-party, 6,800+ apps / 2,500 companies** (Pendo One, May 2023–Mar 2024). TTV by percentile (days): Low 2.6 / Avg 1.5 / High 0.7 / Best-in-class 0.2 (4.8h); best-in-class 7.5× faster; by size Small 1.2/Med 1.6/Large 1.5; Media&Gov fastest 1.1. **Avg 1.5 days corroborates Userpilot ~1d12h.** Other sections (MAU growth, retention, feature adoption, stickiness, guide engagement, NPS) not yet mined. **Caveat:** TTV = avg days to top Core Event (self-defined), vs spec median hours.
- **RevenueCat / Applica — ASL Bloom case** *(via Growth.Design search)* — ✅ verified 2026-11-17: social proof moved off paywall into onboarding (teacher+student). **Redesign** results (+15% trial conv, +12% ARPU, +28% goal, +18% d30 retention) are **not isolated** to social proof; no A/B stated.
